Frequently Asked Questions

How do I track my complaint without logging in?
You can visit the Track Status page at any time and enter your unique 13-digit Complaint ID (e.g. GS-2026-00101) to view current stage and officer remarks.
Who handles Government School infrastructure issues?
Complaints submitted under "Government School Problems" are assigned to the Gram Panchayat Education Committee and jointly coordinated with the Taluk Block Education Officer (BEO) for grant sanction under the 15th Finance Commission or Sarva Shiksha Abhiyan funds.
What is the standard time for streetlight or water repairs?
As per the Karnataka Sakala Civic Services Guarantee guidelines, minor water leaks and streetlight fuse replacements are attended to within 48 to 72 hours. Major civil works (drain reconstruction or road resurfacing) follow budget tender protocols.
Why was my complaint marked as "Rejected"?
A complaint may be closed if it concerns private civil court property disputes, or if duplicate complaints have already been registered for the same location. The exact reason is always noted in the Officer's remarks.
